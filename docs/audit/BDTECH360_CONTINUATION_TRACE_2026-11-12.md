Payment checkout and invoice send have separate paths; `src/app/api/admin/clients/[id]/stage/route.ts` can advance the display stage separately from these functions. A simple stage-only check is insufficient.

The embedded script ignores error status/JSON; decision route does read-then-write and invokes follow-up after persistence; view route can overwrite revision/expired states. New behavior must not rewrite persisted customer history, and must report uncertain follow-up honestly.
- Payments/source: `src/app/api/payments/stripe/checkout/route.ts`, `src/app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts`, `src/app/api/admin/payments/verify/route.ts`, `src/app/api/admin/approvals/[id]/decision/route.ts`, `src/app/api/handover/[token]/download/route.ts`, `src/lib/{commerce-policy,payment-policy,handover-artifacts}.ts`, and `src/app/api/admin/factory/apps/[id]/export/route.ts`. Factory export is Super Admin-only, but generates a customer-labelled ZIP without a payment check; it is not the public handover endpoint. Currency and refunds must be handled before claiming universally safe payment totals.
- WhatsApp: `src/app/api/webhooks/whatsapp/route.ts`, `src/lib/{whatsapp-webhook,identity-policy,identity-resolver,journey,comms,chat-orchestrator}.ts`. Signed messages are claimed by `claimInboundWebhookEvent` with a fenced lease; successful claims are completed via `finishInboundWebhookEvent`. Only the first entry/change is traversed; errors and rate limiting acknowledge 200; failed receipt claims are reclaimable, but active claims and already-processed receipts are indistinguishable to callers. Processing may perform CRM writes and sends before receipt finalization, so retrying naively may duplicate outbound actions. Status updates have no event receipt. Provider policy and live redelivery behavior are unverified here.

### Operational and security limits
- `ScopeApproval` does not identify or authenticate the customer who supplied a decision. Operators/n8n can record it; this is evidence of a recorded decision, not cryptographic proof of customer identity. Historical evidence must not be invented; missing scope/preview evidence now blocks the affected operations and existing issued invoices without evidence cannot use Stripe checkout. Review compatibility before rollout.
- Provider acceptance plus a tokenized view is stronger than a send attempt, but is not proof a particular customer read the preview; token forwarding and automated link scanning are possible. Client-only scope versions/project association and all possible custom/manual payment channels have not been exhaustively proven safe. Gate reads are not serialized with subsequent sends or concurrently changing scope/payment/refund state. Production MySQL concurrency testing and an authenticated approval/evidence model remain necessary.
- A preview decision transaction is conditional, but the follow-up remains outside the transaction. Failure produces `followUpStatus=NEEDS_REVIEW`; there is no durable follow-up outbox or exactly-once guarantee. Unknown legacy preview scripts are intentionally untouched. DB integration test exercises sequential repeated requests, not true MySQL concurrent decisions.
- WhatsApp receipts fence claims but cannot fence a slow worker's external actions after lease expiry. A stored inbound replay is retained and flagged for manual review; a partial intake may have other incomplete side effects. Provider redelivery behavior and live outbound sends were not tested. Status callbacks are not ordered monotonically.
- Hostinger Mail, scheduler, AI, Gmail and other live integrations were not exercised or validated. Gmail remains separate. Factory Super Admin export is not the customer bearer-link handover download; review all privileged/internal routes and arbitrary communications before claiming universal release or payment enforcement.
- Rollback: revert the changed application and test files on this branch through a normal reviewed commit, leaving existing database records intact. No schema changes or migration rollback are required. Restoring old application code also restores the previously identified bypasses, so assess and restrict affected actions until an alternate fix is deployed.
